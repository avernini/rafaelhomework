Reforço de Fixação: Verbo To Be

Complete os blocos de exercícios práticos abaixo para consolidar o uso do verbo To Be (afirmativa, negativa e interrogativa) no contexto de rotina e personalidade. Cada bloco contém 5 desafios.

Exercise 1: Affirmative Sentences

5 Questions

1. I ______ very creative when I design digital interfaces.

2. My colleagues ______ busy with meetings this afternoon.

3. The new software ______ essential for our daily routine.

4. We ______ focused on the new marketing project right now.

5. He ______ an energetic person, especially in the morning.

Exercise 2: Negative Sentences

5 Questions

1. She ______ stressed because her schedule is quite flexible.

2. I ______ tired today; I have plenty of energy for the gym.

3. They ______ happy with the new office layout.

4. It ______ easy to balance work and personal life all the time.

5. You ______ late for the team meeting, don't worry.

Exercise 3: Formulating Questions

5 Questions

1. "______ you relaxed on weekends, or do you still check emails?"

2. "______ fitness important for your lifestyle?"

3. "Where ______ your friends right now?"

4. "______ I on the right track with this new assignment?"

5. "______ he usually quiet during brainstorming sessions?"
