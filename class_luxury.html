Step 3: Grammar Calibration (10-12 min)

Grammar Focus: Expressing Preferences & Comparisons (Near B1)

To talk about properties and lifestyle choices effectively, master comparative structures and preference verbs:

1. Expressing Preferences (Would rather / prefer)

• I would rather live in a quiet countryside estate.

• She prefers living in a secure gated community.

2. Advanced Comparisons (As... as / Much more...)

• This mansion is much more expensive than the previous one.

• Privacy is as important as location.

Interactive Grammar Training (4 Challenges)

1. "I would ______ buy a secluded house than a busy downtown apartment."

2. "Luxury gated communities are ______ secure than normal neighborhoods."

3. "Investing in real estate is ______ profitable than keeping cash."

4. "A custom renovation is ______ costly than buying a ready-to-move house."
